TASK 1: Academic Writing Task 1 — Household internet access
INSTRUCTION:
You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

The table below shows the percentage of households with a fixed internet
connection in four Indonesian cities in 2015 and in 2025.

City               Year 2015     Year 2025
Medan                    31%           74%
Surabaya                 38%           81%
Makassar                 22%           69%
Denpasar                 27%           77%

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and
make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words. Do not copy whole sentences from the task. You may
write in note form first, but only what appears in the answer box is marked.
